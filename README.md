# ReviseAI

ReviseAI helps students make the most of their study time. Add your course notes to create a clear summary, an interactive quiz, or both, then use the results to review key ideas and check your understanding.

Choose a difficulty that suits you, answer the quiz at your own pace, and download a corrected study sheet to keep for later. ReviseAI is designed to make studying feel more focused, approachable, and effective.
